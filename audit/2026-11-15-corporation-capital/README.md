# When a public Tobacco corporation should reinvest after v2

A `MAX` order is a quantity instruction. The signed rate next to a product in the warehouse is inventory change (manufactured minus sold), and an asking price is not revenue. Both products can sell out between observations. The reported BN3.3 screen showed $975.229b in company funds, $2.10m/s net across the corporation, and effective ratings of 62.843 and 63.437 in one city. A single screenshot does not establish sell-through, the design city's size, or a product's earnings across all cities.

**What to buy.** In Steam 3.0.1 the design contribution to raw rating is `1 + designInvestment^0.1 / 100`. Raising $1b to $2b changes that multiplier by 0.53%; raising it to $100b changes it by 4.3%. Marketing has a similarly small exponent in markup and demand. Neither slider determines factory output or design speed. A product's stats use the designing office's productivity, weighted over the entire design, and research at completion. Tobacco weights quality, durability and aesthetics 70/10/20. Plants can cap the rating actually used for sales. See the game's [product model](https://github.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/blob/3162fd2590e221eadd0c0fbd46151913f7c4c41c/src/Corporation/Product.ts#L109-L220) and [production and effective rating](https://github.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/blob/3162fd2590e221eadd0c0fbd46151913f7c4c41c/src/Corporation/Division.ts#L848-L946).

The previous manager funded a special case for v2. After v2 it valued a design-office expansion only for this city's current sales and required a ten-minute payback. Its price calibration also multiplied the product's API market price by five again. Doubling the next design budget or assuming a momentary zero was a sales failure would address neither issue.

**What changed for a public, managed Tobacco line with at least two finished products:**

- Keep selling `MAX`. Before Market-TA.II, use the game's price for selling the stored quantity: `MP + markupLimit × sqrt(potentialSales / targetSales)`. The API field `productionCost` already equals the displayed **market** price. The five published finished-product stats let us recover the Business/Management contribution to markup without knowing historical RP or reading a save. The order uses an `MP+` expression, so the input price can change between the quote and the sale. With native TA.II, let the game set the price. If market data or stats are missing or the job mix is indeterminate, retain the existing fallback. [Sale formula](https://github.com/bitburner-official/bitburner-src/blob/3162fd2590e221eadd0c0fbd46151913f7c4c41c/src/Corporation/Division.ts#L263-L440).
- At a three-person Sector-12 office, compare one Operations, one Engineer and one Business against one Operations, one Engineer and one Management. Move the existing worker only if the observed output and sales formula support at least as much current city profit. The management assignment can speed the design without buying a seat. No manual reassignment is required.
- Compare three more headquarters seats with three seats in a satellite that would get its first researcher. Model input quality, measured unit sales, the city's business output, advertising, research and Tobacco's rating weights. Use up to six settled cycles of income and volume, with at least three observations and two distinct selling products. Discount projected gains by half after dividends. Reserve bills, inputs, the next project and bribe cash. The extra budget can initially use up to one quarter of uncommitted cash for that finished lineup; half of new positive earnings remains protected. Recheck cash, bills, city, lineup, phase and price in the same home call as the purchase.
- Only pay if the projected increase covers the cost before the faction cash target or expected node finish. For a run forecast, use the median time to the finish-ready milestone in the last three completed runs of this same BitNode that recorded it, accounting for time already elapsed. If none are available, look ahead only far enough to develop a lineup of products at current staffing. Without a fresh eligible faction target, also cap the comparison at the time to earn back today's spare cash. The forecast is deliberately conservative: it credits successive fully improved designs once per design period, and only as many as there are product slots. Spending after a project begins cannot retroactively change its accumulated stats.

**Measured comparisons.** Both versions run the actual manager, Steam's office productivity, product development, production, sales and upgrade-price formulas. The low-income setup uses six small offices (assumed, not read from the screenshot), the same two existing sellers and a v3 already 15% developed. Both start with $975.229b; other divisions contribute identical net income within each comparison, calibrated so the *previous* corporation starts around $2.10m/s. The run estimate is 19 hours, dividends 1%, target $1t for the faction plus the operating reserve. We measure when cash is available, without executing the bribe.

| At normal game speed | Previous | Updated |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| v3 time remaining (both pricing modes) | 143.8 min | 110.7 min |
| v3 raw rating (both pricing modes) | 64.2 | 76.4 |
| Tobacco net, script pricing | $0.313m/s | $0.952m/s |
| $1t bribe affordable, script pricing | 236.2 min | 187.3 min |
| $1t bribe affordable, native Market-TA.II | 226.7 min | 219.0 min |
| Extra office spend in this comparison | $0 | $0 |

The opposite case was an established line with a 30-person headquarters, five 12-person satellites, 124k RP, higher-quality Plants and an $8t faction target. With the new pricing and staffing but discretionary design-seat purchases disabled, Tobacco earned $412.14m/s and the company held $18.132t after 12 hours. Enabling those purchases spent $537.5b over the design period, raised v3's rating from 4,450.5 to 7,578.0, and yielded $690.26m/s and **$28.807t** in cash at the same 12-hour mark. The fixture holds the market, ingredient quality and other division earnings fixed, makes only office upgrades competitively priced, and stops buying seats or starting more products after v3 finishes to isolate the change. These are comparisons, not forecasts for a specific save or a claim that hiring always wins.

**In the game:** `run git-pull.js --branch testing/bn1-go-audit`, then stop the old `corporation.js` and let autopilot relaunch it. The company, current design and sales remain in place. The corporation log shows Tobacco net, each product's actual units sold per second across all cities averaged over market cycles, and the last expansion or cash-holding decision. Manager and regular home helper still launch at 5.50 and 23.60 GB. A guarded expansion briefly needs 61.60 GB on home, the existing peak; if that is unavailable it defers.
